Building upon seminal works in the history of computational art, I created this series of creative coding explorations to investigate how developing technologies shape artistic expression. This body of work began as part of Zach Lieberman's class Recreating the past (MAS.S69).


[1] Layer Lines, based on the work of Steina and Woody Vasulka.

Link ↗
[2] Text Storm, after Text Rain by Camille Utterback; I implemented a lightweight physics engine and edge detection to manipulate the falling letters.

Link ↗
[3] Dynam1c Supremat1sm, after Dynamic Suprematism by Kazimir Malevich. I was interested in the dissolution of shape, line, and plane in geometric abstraction painting, and how this could be mirrored with modern SVG manipulation techniques.

Link ↗
[4] Icon-cam, after Ken Knowlton's work. A mapping of the self onto the Google Material Icons library.
