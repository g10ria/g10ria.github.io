Mit Gala - Printed Matter


Discipline

Graphic Design, Branding

Date

2023–2026

Mediums
  • Adobe Illustrator
  • Adobe Photoshop
  • Laser-cutting


As design director of MIT Gala, MIT's undergraduate fashion show, I maintained a cohesive brand identity each year and designed a variety of printed matter: posters, tickets, flyers, signage, and stickers.



[1] The program for MIT Gala 2025, which was themed around the ocean and bodies of water. I went with a maximalist aesthetic, combining digital collage techniques with hand-drawn and scanned textural elements.
[2] Admit-one tickets for MIT Gala 2024, manually printed and laser-cut to create unique silhouettes and graphic permutations.
[3] Pages from an earlier MIT Gala program.
[4] Posters promoting community events and opportunities.
