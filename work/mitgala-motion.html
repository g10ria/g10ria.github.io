Mit Gala - Motion Graphics


Discipline

Motion Design, Interactive Visuals

Date

2023–2026

Mediums
  • Adobe After Effects
  • Adobe Illustrator
  • p5.js


As design director of MIT Gala, MIT's undergraduate fashion show, I created motion graphics and microsites to market the yearly event and interactive visualizations to play in the background of the runway.

See also: MIT Gala - Printed Matter


[1] Projected water visuals for the 2025 show, playing behind the runway. I adapted a WebGL fluid simulation and added optimizations, MIDI input to sync the visualization to the music, and keyboard controls for live VJ-ing.
[2] An animated call for designers to apply to design for MIT Gala.
[3] An animated Instagram story graphic to remind attendees of the the show's date and location.
